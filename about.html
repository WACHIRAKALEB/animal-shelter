Our story, our promise

History of Upendo Animal Shelter

Upendo animal shelter was founded in 2015 by a group of animal lovers who were concerned about the welfare of animals in their community. They saw the need for a safe haven for abandoned and injured animals, and decided to take action. Over the years, Upendo has grown into a well-respected organization that has helped countless animals find loving homes.

Upendo Adoption

Upendo animal shelter is dedicated to finding loving homes for the animals in our care. We believe that every animal deserves a second chance at life, and we work tirelessly to ensure that they receive the care and attention they need. Our adoption process is designed to match animals with the right families, and we provide ongoing support to ensure that the transition is smooth for both the animal and their new family.

Here at upendo animal shelter we give out the rescued animals for adoption to the willing families provided they have a home for the adopted animal and are capable of feeding it and providing the required medical attention to the pet when required to do so. Below are some of the reqirements needed for a succesful adoption process

  • A legal ID
  • suitable housing for the pet
  • Financial responsibility for the pet's care
  • Commitment to the pet's long-term welfare
  • household permission if one lives in a rented house

Upendo Pro

Upendo pro simply means that there are animal or household pets which are higher in terms of adoption fee since this pets have to be bought first, this is because they were not rescued or returned by previous owners but have rather been imported from different parts of the globe.We charge a high amount due to their importation fee and feeding program some of them eat specific meals.

Those who are in consideration of adopting the imported pets we have to check for several other things apart from the ordinary adoption requirements.This is

  • financial stability to cater for the pet's needs
  • Experience with the specific type of pet
  • Ability to provide proper care and attention
  • Space and resources to accommodate the pet
