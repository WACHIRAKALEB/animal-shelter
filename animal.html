Upendo Rabbits

Most of the rabbits are not rescued ones rather they are imported from different places of the Globe and so they are in excellent medical conditions.Our breeds include holland lop,Netherland dwarf, Flemish giant,New Zealand and English Angora.The rabbits are not given out at a normal adoption fee rather they are primarily sold inclusive of the adoption fee.
